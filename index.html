About me

I like being the person who makes technology less of a problem for everyone else.

Much of what I know about technology came from working close to the people who depend on it. My first job in the field was in IT support and network administration, where my days went on fixing faults, setting up access and keeping equipment accounted for.

From there I moved into AI data work, helping train models by labelling and judging data against strict guidelines. It taught me how much a system depends on careful, consistent human attention. Technical writing came next, and with it the lesson that a clear explanation is often the real fix.

Across all three, I work the same way. I find out what the person on the other end actually needs, solve the problem properly, and leave a clear record behind so the next person does not have to start from zero.

I am looking for a role in IT support, technical operations or data work, ideally on a team that values patience, accuracy and good documentation.

What I do

Keep systems running

I troubleshoot hardware, software and network problems, set up user accounts and permissions, and help watch networks so small faults get caught early.

Build and code

I write in C, C++, Java and Kotlin, and build web pages with HTML5 and CSS. My support work keeps me thinking about the person using the finished thing.

Prepare data for AI

I annotate, classify and evaluate data that is used to train AI models, and review search results for relevance. The work rewards accuracy and consistency, and I enjoy both.

Explain it clearly

I research technical and academic subjects and turn them into guides, manuals and structured documents, then edit and proofread until they meet the standard the reader expects.
